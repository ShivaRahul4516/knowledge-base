How do I connect my text files?
  1. Create one folder on your PC, for example Knowledge Base Content.
  2. Put your .txt or .md files inside it. You can also organise them into subfolders.
  3. Click Connect CONTENT FOLDER and choose that folder.
  4. Use Import .txt/.md files when you want to bring existing notes into the Knowledge Base.
  5. Use Export content when you want a backup of your articles as files.

Recommended: keep the files in one dedicated folder and use subfolders for modules such as Browse, Workflow, Manage, etc.
