How do I connect my text files?
  1. Create a folder on your PC for your knowledge base.
  2. Put your TXT, Markdown or DOCX files inside it.
  3. Click Connect CONTENT FOLDER and choose that folder.
  4. Use Upload documents to add DOCX, text documents and images.
  5. Use Export content to create a backup.

Supported: TXT, Markdown, DOCX and common image formats. Legacy DOC files must be converted to DOCX first.
